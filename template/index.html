Frequently Asked Questions

📝 How do I register?

Click on the "Join Community" button in the top right or in the hero section, fill in your details, and start using the platform.

🔍 How can I search for estates?

Once logged in, use the search bar on the homepage to find estates by name or location.

⭐ How do I write a review?

Click on the "Write Review" button under an estate card and submit your feedback using the form.

How do I place a Reservation?

Click on "Place Reservation" under an estate and send your request.

📞 How do I contact you?

Click on the "Contact" link in the navbar to send us your message via the contact form.
